# Knight · Skeleton · Robot – active ragdoll sandbox

Code-driven animation for three rigged characters, no Blender: `Untitled.glb` (the knight), `skeleton.glb` and `robot.glb` (the robot, whose signature move is the **jump**). All three are the same "SmartRig" layout, so one engine (ghost pose + IK + active ragdoll + walker) runs them; they share one physics world and push each other around. You drive one of them, the other two are **NPCs: neutral until you hit them, then they fight back** (and calm down again). Physics = Rapier, rendering = three.js, everything vendored (works offline).

**Run:** double-click `start.bat` (needs Node), or `node server.js` and open <http://localhost:5173>.

**Controls** (the same scheme on every character; the moves behind the keys differ, the panel on the left lists them for whoever you drive):
- **WASD / arrows** walk (relative to the camera; Shift = slow for the knight, **run** for the skeleton / robot) · **Tab** (or the *Play:* buttons) = who you drive
- **Left click** = the light attack (hold = the combo keeps going; `J` does the same from the keyboard) · **E** = heavy attack · **Q / R** = special moves · **F** = a third special (the knight: draw / sheathe the sword) · **Space** = jump (robot; hold a direction to leap) / keep the guard up (hold, knight and skeleton)
- **Right-drag** = orbit, **wheel** = zoom · **Ctrl+click** (or a touch tap) = shove the character under the pointer (shift = hard)
- Fighting standing still, you turn toward the nearest foe by yourself (aim assist, within 5 m)
- `Ragdoll amount` slider (default 25%) · Knock down / Stand up now / Reset · "get up automatically" · "NPCs fight back when hit" (off = the other two just stand there)

| | click (combo) | E (heavy) | Q | R | F |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Knight**, sheathed | jab - cross - hook - cross | haymaker (knocks down) | uppercut (launcher) | elbow strike | draw sword |
| **Knight**, sword drawn | forehand - backhand cuts | overhead chop (knocks down) | lunging two-handed thrust | rising cut (launcher) | sheathe |
| **Skeleton** | slash - hook - backhand - hook | two-handed smash | spear-hand lunge stab | double claw rake (an X) | rising claw (launcher) |
| **Robot** | piston - piston - piston - sweep - chop | hammer blow | piston barrage (7 rapid pistons) | launching uppercut | thunder clap (two hands, knocks down) |

Light moves shove, heavy moves and launchers knock the target down. The NPCs use their specials too (now and then, at close range).

## How it works
| layer | file | job |
|---|---|---|
| rig definition | `src/humanoid.js`, `src/profile.json`, `src/profile-skeleton.json`, `src/profile-robot.json` | which physics body drives which bones, joint types/limits/strengths; colliders fitted to the mesh by `tools/build-profile.mjs` |
| **ghost** | `src/ghost.js` | kinematic *target pose* generator: keyframes + 2-bone IK + ground contact. The walker, the jump and the upper-body layers write their pose into its parameter vector |
| **active ragdoll** | `src/knight.js` | 16 Rapier bodies; joint torque controllers chase the ghost; soft balance assist at the pelvis (+ foot springs and a damped chest/head hold while walking / jumping); **self-collision** between limbs, torso and head; `fall → getup → stand` state machine; the drawn pose is interpolated between physics steps (smooth at any refresh rate). (The class is called `Knight` for historical reasons; every character is one) |
| **walk** | `src/walk.js` | footstep planner + gait phase: feet are planted in the world and swing along minimum-jerk arcs to the point where the pelvis will be at mid-stance; pelvis bob, a little sway/roll, shoulders turning against the hips, steady helmet and arm swing all come from the same phase; speed and heading follow smoothed responses the planner predicts; WALK → RUN blend; the robot's *style* makes all of it mechanical (see Robot) |
| **jump** | `src/jump.js` | the robot's jump: a kinematic ghost move (squat, push-off, ballistic flight, landing absorb, recover) plus the physics support that actually throws and catches the body (see Jump) |
| rigs | `src/rigs.js` | one spec per character: bone names, collision-proxy shapes, mass / inertia tweaks, layer (sword or attack), gait style, jump, collision bit. The ghost, walker and controllers read the proportions from the rig (`rigScale`) |
| **sword** | `src/sword.js`, `sword and sword holder.glb` | the holder hangs from the belt at the left side of the waist (attached to the pelvis, with a hanger strap + collar), sword in the holder / right hand, **two-handed** guard once drawn (both unskinned meshes). `SwordLayer` drives the ghost (torso twist, hand IK targets, elbow pole) so the physics body performs the cross-draw: the left hand carries the scabbard while the right hand pulls the sword; standing, the left hand rests on the pommel. Fingers curl around the grip (`skin.js`). It also hosts the fist layer of characters without a sword, so every character has the same `attack()` interface |
| **attack** | `src/attack.js` | fist swings as an upper-body layer (same interface as the sword layer): coil → whip → follow-through. The skeleton's (slash, hook, two-handed overhead smash, thrown while it keeps running), the knight's punches (jab, cross, hook, haymaker) and the robot's (piston = a straight linear thrust, sweep, hammer). Each swing twists / leans the torso (a critically damped follower: a violent target must not whip the torso), drives the hand, clenches the fingers (`curl`) and turns the hand over by 180° about the forearm as it lands (`roll`, the corkscrew of a real punch) |
| **combat** | `src/combat.js` | who hits whom, for all characters: a swing in its strike window whose hand / forearm (or the knight's blade, a sensor capsule on the right hand) touches a body of another character lands once: a shove at the contact point, a heavy blow also knocks it down. It reports every landed blow through `combat.onHit({att, tgt, s, point, heavy, part})` (the NPC brains and the camera shake listen to it). Exact shape queries (`contactCollider`), run after every physics step |
| **npc** | `src/npc.js` | the brain of every character you do not drive (see NPC behaviour) |
| skin / scene | `src/skin.js`, `src/arena.js`, `src/main.js`, `index.html` | writes body rotations onto the bones; tiny arena; UI |

`ragdoll` (0–1) scales joint strength and the balance assist: ~0–25% stands tall, 50–80% slumps more and more, 100% stays limp.

## Smoothness of the combat (what was measured and fixed)
`node tools/combat-smooth.mjs [fists|sword|heavy|walk|mixed|special] [seconds]` (env `RIG=`, `KEYS=QRF` for the specials) measures the ghost's pops (accelerations of the hands / arm joints in one step) and the physical jitter; `node tools/flip-trace.mjs [seconds] [thr]` (env `RIG=`, `MIXED=1`, `SWORD=1`) finds one-step flips of the ghost's arm joints and prints what the layer asked for. Before / after on the same scripted fights: the skeleton's upper-arm angular acceleration p99 39,000 -> ~1,500 rad/s2 (max 43,000 -> ~2,500), the knight's sword combo p99 18,900 -> ~350 (hand jitter 220 -> 140, head jitter 47 -> 12), the robot's hammer pops 37,000 -> ~1,900, hand error vs the ghost 11 -> 7 cm (skeleton). Causes and fixes:
- **the hand passing through the shoulder** (a cock behind the body cross-faded with the end of a blow across it are opposite directions: the elbow IK has no defined bend there): `AttackLayer.refresh` blends the hand targets in polar form (directions + radii), keeps a smooth minimum reach, and routes a cross-fade of two moves through the guard; the ghost blends the layer's hand into the walker's along the shoulder's sphere (`blendReach`)
- **an arm that is nearly straight** (the two-handed sword guard) flipped about its axis between a forehand and a backhand cut: `SwordLayer.limitReach` (soft reach limit at ~92%)
- **a bend side (pole) antiparallel to the reach** (overhead blows): the twist's authority fades out where the pole is (anti)parallel to the reach, the twist angle is tracked through time (no +-PI wrap), the walker's pole and the layer's are blended along the shortest arc (`slerpDir`); `pole: 'free'` in a move table = the walker's own elbow (smoothest for the robot and for the skeleton's two-handed moves), the knight's punches keep explicit poles
- the elbow side follows its target like a critically damped spring; a landed blow slows the attacker's own move clock for 35 / 70 ms (`hitStop`, weight)
- the **robot's gait** (`ROBOT.style` in `rigs.js`, found with `tools/style-scan.mjs`): a less boxy foot path, a slower metronome and a higher lift; the feet used to slam down at ~1 m/s (mean) and now land at ~0.1 m/s, foot tracking error 6.6 -> 5.7 cm, foot slip 0.9 -> 0.4 m/s

## NPC behaviour (`src/npc.js`)
Every character you are not driving has a brain (`NpcAI`), and by default it is **neutral**: it stands there (the robot's head glances around, the others idle) and never starts anything. States: `calm → fight → (stunned) → gloat → calm`.
- **Provoked** by `combat.onHit` (a landed punch / swing / blade) or by a click-shove. It turns on whoever did it, opening with quick blows (its heavy move only after a moment), and fights with whatever the character has: the **skeleton** runs at you and swings savagely from ~4 m out; the **robot** runs, piston-punches, hits with the hammer now and then, leaps across a gap (3.4 – 9 m) and sometimes hops out of the way of a swing that is coming; the **knight** defends himself with fists (a heavy blow knocks the NPC back, light ones only shove it, so it keeps trading blows), and while the attacker is down or knocked away he stops to **draw his sword** and uses it afterwards.
- A heavy blow stops it for ~0.9 s (cancels its swing); light ones do not stun-lock it. If the attacker is down it waits (`gloat`) for it to get up instead of kicking it.
- **Calms down** again 9 s after the last hit, or when the attacker is more than 14 m away; the knight sheathes his sword after 5 more seconds of peace.
- The AI of the character you drive is switched off (`state: player`); `Tab` hands control over and the previous one becomes an NPC. The "NPCs fight back when hit" checkbox turns the whole thing off (then nobody ever fights back).
- The label under the title shows each NPC's state.

## Robot (`ROBOT` in `src/rigs.js`)
`robot.glb` (53 bones, 116k vertices, 1.7 m, T-pose facing +Z) fitted by `tools/build-profile.mjs robot.glb src/profile-robot.json robot`: same 16 bodies, slimmer collision proxies (thighs / arms must not rub), heavier virtual inertia floors on every limb, the 3 × 4 finger chains of each hand curl as a unit. **Movement is robotic** (the gait *style* in `ROBOT.style`, all in the same walker): no breathing sway, the idle head **servo-scans** (holds a glance for 2.7 s then snaps to the next within 0.2 s), feet follow a box-shaped path (lift straight up, travel flat, drop straight down, softly) with a hinge-like heel-toe roll, the torso is carried as a rigid block (no bob / sway / bank to speak of), the arms swing stiff and straight and only part of the way (`armReach`, `armSwing`), the head follows a turn in snaps (`headSnap`), a wider stance and constant-speed steps (`mech`, `rigid`, `Tmin`). Its run has the same stiffness with the legs pumping faster.

## Jump (`src/jump.js`) — the robot's signature ability
Space (`ctl.ch.jump.request(dir, power)`): a short squat, an explosive push-off, a ballistic flight, a stiff-legged landing that absorbs the fall, a quick recover. Standing: the pelvis rises ~1 m (about a metre of air under the feet, ~1.4 s from squat to standing again). Holding a direction leaps ~4 m forward (and turns the robot toward it during the squat); jumping while running keeps the running momentum. The robot can chain jumps (35 ms cooldown after landing) and turns in the air. How it works: the ghost does the whole move as a kinematic target (horizontal speed carried by the ghost origin, a hand-built pelvis-height curve, legs / arms / torso written into the ghost's parameter vector), and the physics body gets what a jump needs on top of the normal controllers: the pelvis spring caps raised to several times body weight (`support`) and a mass-proportional feed-forward on *every* body during the launch (otherwise the legs push the torso up and it folds ~80° at the waist), foot springs, and the damped chest / head hold during and after (the torso is a heavy pendulum on a light waist joint). The NPC robot uses it to close a gap and to dodge.

## Turning
Heading follows a critically damped third-order response (no overshoot, finite jerk; peak turn acceleration ~5 rad/s², jerk ~80 at speed, ~8 / ~130 pivoting on the spot). The head and chest lead the turn by a smoothed error (they used to snap when a key was pressed), the pelvis banks into it, and speed eases off to ~2/3 for a 90° turn. `node tools/turn-check.mjs [knight|skeleton|robot]` (env `RUN=1` to run) measures it.

## Running (skeleton, robot)
The walker blends WALK → RUN by the speed it really has (`gait` 0..1, over 1.25–2 m/s): 3.2 m/s top speed, shorter ground contact (a flight phase), the pelvis dips at mid-stance (the walk's cosine shifted half a period), ~17° forward lean with the chin up, bent pumping arms, high knees, banking into turns. A style without `run` (the knight) just walks. `command(dir, throttle, run)`.

## Skeleton notes
Engine-wise the skeleton is the knight with other data (`src/rigs.js`): slimmer collision proxies, a lighter arm mass (a heavy arm whipped through a swing folds the torso), larger chest / abdomen / head inertia floors (the joint controllers are implicit-Euler scaled per joint, so several stiff controllers stacked on one light body — joint PD + the walking torso hold + arm reaction — ring at the step rate and the torso flips over; heavier virtual inertia fixes that), hinge-keyed leg IK (its rest legs are knock-kneed), a small keyframe tweak so the back knee clears the floor in the get-up lunge, less pelvis sway (wide hips). It stands up, walks, runs, turns, falls and gets up like the knight. Its swings only reach ~40 cm, so the AI closes to ~60 cm (pelvis to pelvis) before it hacks. The arena is a 12 m platform with a curb and an invisible wall.

## Sword notes
Draw = the left hand takes the scabbard throat (carrying it toward the right hand and swivelling it), the right hand reaches across to the hilt (torso twists) → grips → pulls along the holder axis → swings up to a two-handed guard; sheathe is the reverse. While in the holder the sword can only slide along the holder axis (following the physical hand), then it is attached to the hand. Standing still the left hand rests on the pommel; walking it swings freely. The empty scabbard hangs from the belt (it droops by gravity when he is crawling or lying, and neither it nor the blade sinks into the floor). Knocked down mid-draw it ends up sheathed or in hand. The parameters (holder placement, draw directions, torso offsets, guard, left-hand rest) live in `TUNE` and were found with `tools/sword-tune.mjs`, which keeps every body part out of every other (collision shapes), the blade / scabbard / hilt out of the body, the joints inside their limits and both hands on target; `node tools/apply-tune.mjs <output>` writes a result into `sword.js`.

## Walking notes
Speed up to 1.2 m/s (accelerates/decelerates smoothly), ~140 steps/min at full speed, stride grows with speed, turns gently while striding and quickly when almost standing (a U-turn is nearly a pivot), stops by settling into the idle stance. The torso stays steady: the chest and head are held on the ghost's orientation by a damped hand-of-god torque while he walks (left to the joint controllers the soft torso rings at the step rate), the pelvis only sways ~1 cm. While he is blocked (wall, shove) the ghost is kept within 30 cm of the physical pelvis, so he just marches on the spot. Draw/sheathe need him standing still (pressing F while walking stops him first). The balance assist uses the ghost's root velocity as feed-forward while standing/walking; the feet are pulled to the ghost's feet by capped springs so landings happen where they were planned and planted feet do not skate.

## Attacks
**Knight, sword sheathed:** punches (`PUNCHES` in `attack.js`): jab (left) - cross (right) - hook (left) - cross, haymaker = the heavy one (knocks down). The left hand leaves the pommel, the fists clench, hips / shoulders turn into it. **Sword drawn:** forehand (right to left) and backhand cuts, alternating; heavy = the overhead chop (knocks down). The sword is placed in the chest frame (`SWORD_SWINGS` in `sword.js`: hand centre + blade azimuth / elevation), so the twisting torso carries it; the left hand follows the grip as always. A press during a swing is buffered into the next one; a queued heavy attack is not bumped by a held light one. `tools/atk-check.mjs` checks the reach and joint limits of every move kinematically, `tools/knight-attack.mjs` runs them in the physics. **Skeleton:** its fingers are procedural (the model only has palm bones: `spec.fingers` in `rigs.js`, `skin.js` adds chained finger bones and moves the finger vertices' weights onto them); relaxed hands curl a little, a swing clenches them into a fist and turns them 180° (palm up at the wind-up, palm down at the impact). **Robot** (`ROBOT_SWINGS`): *piston* = a straight, mechanical thrust along a line (`lin`), alternating arms, *sweep* = a flat horizontal arm sweep, *hammer* (heavy) = both fists raised and brought down; its combo is piston L - piston R - piston L - sweep R.
A few things the controllers needed (found with per-source torque logs, `tools/torque-log.mjs`): the knight's torso / arm inertia floors were raised (stacked stiff controllers on a light body ring), the arms' damper and hand-of-god springs track the ghost's velocity while a swing drives them (no lag behind a fast hand), and the torso follows the swing through a critically damped follower.

## Collisions
All characters share one physics world: each one's fitted colliders touch the world and the other characters' (never its own), so they shove each other (collision bits 15 knight / 13 skeleton / 12 robot in `knight.js`, bits 0–11 are the self-collision proxies, 14 is the world). Limbs, torso and head collide with each other through slimmer proxy shapes (`PROXY` in `src/humanoid.js`, per-rig overrides in `rigs.js`; the fitted colliders include flared cloth and still only touch the world). Pairs that are jointed neighbours, or that overlap by design, are listed in `NOHIT`; groups are in `GROUPS`. `?col=1` draws the shapes over the knight, `knight.setSelfCollision(false)` switches them off, `node tools/prox-check.mjs` reports the clearance between every pair in the idle / walk / turn / draw / guard / get-up poses and `node tools/contact-log.mjs [walk|sword|guard|fall|getup|run|swing]` which parts actually touch in the physics run.

## Dev tools (headless, no browser)
- Most tools take the character from env `RIG=skeleton|robot` (default knight): `RIG=robot node tools/walk-sim.mjs`, `RIG=skeleton node tools/fuzz-getup.mjs 8 1 walk`, ... (`turn-check.mjs` takes it as an argument).
- `PASSIVE=1 node tools/npc-test.mjs ...` = the victims do not fight back (the attacker's own hit rate); `node tools/npc-test.mjs <attacker> <victim> [fists|sword]` (attacker / victim: `knight|skeleton|robot`; env `TRACE=1` prints every state change) – three characters in one world, one scripted as the "player": 12 s of nobody attacking (the NPCs must stay calm, zero hits), then it hits the victim for 28 s (the victim must turn on it and fight back), then it stops (the victim must calm down again; a knight sheathes his sword)
- `RIG=robot node tools/jump-sim.mjs [up|leap|run|chain|turn] [verbose]` – the robot's jumps in the physics (apex, distance, landing, ends standing); `RIG=robot node tools/style-scan.mjs 'mech=0.7,lift=0.06' 'mech=0.85' ...` – try gait-style variants (foot tracking error, touchdown speed, wobble); `node tools/glb-info.mjs file.glb` – bones / meshes / bounds of a model
- `node tools/sim-test.mjs forward|back|side [ragdoll] [seconds]` – full knock-down → get-up timeline
- `node tools/ghost-check.mjs` – kinematic keyframe sanity (reach errors, ground contact)
- `node tools/walk-check.mjs` – kinematic gait check (foot reach, bob, slip, stop, turn)
- `node tools/walk-sim.mjs [ragdoll] [sword]` – physics run: walk → turn → stop (with the sword drawn if `sword`)
- `node tools/sword-sim.mjs [ragdoll] [interrupt]` – physics run of draw → hold → sheathe, with hand tracking errors
- `node tools/sword-tune.mjs [iterations] [seed]` (env `BOUNDS="mouth.0:0.19:0.26,..."`) – re-search the draw parameters (reach, joint limits, collision-free arms / blade / scabbard); `node tools/apply-tune.mjs out.txt` writes the best set into `src/sword.js`
- `node tools/smooth-check.mjs [thr=0.5] [nosc] [spikes]` – how smooth is the walk (high-frequency acceleration of pelvis / chest / head / hands / feet, torso wobble, foot error vs the ghost, touchdown speeds); `node tools/gait-scan.mjs scan` kinematic scan of the pelvis-bob settings; `node tools/perf.mjs [sword]` cost of one physics step
- `node tools/prox-check.mjs`, `node tools/contact-log.mjs` – self-collision clearance (kinematic) and contacts (physics), see Collisions
- `node tools/fuzz-getup.mjs [trials] [seed] [plain|walk|sword]` – knock him over in random directions / strengths and check he stands up (run several seeds in parallel after changing the physics: the get-up is sensitive)
- `RIG=skeleton node tools/run-sim.mjs [ragdoll] [swing] [verbose]` – physics run: stand → run → (frenzy swings) → turn → stop; `RIG=skeleton node tools/foot-speed.mjs` – ghost vs physical foot speeds while running; `RIG=skeleton [MODE=run|walk|stand] [LEAN=1] node tools/swing-debug.mjs [t0] [t1] [ragdoll] [variants]` – torso angles (physical vs ghost) during swings
- `RIG=skeleton node tools/fuzz-run.mjs [trials] [seed]` – knock the skeleton (or `RIG=robot`) over mid-run (with / without swings) and check it gets up and runs again
- `node tools/fuzz-attack.mjs [trials] [seed]` – knock the knight over in the middle of punches / sword swings and check he gets up and can attack again
- `node tools/knight-attack.mjs [punch|sword|heavy|chop] [seconds] [ragdoll] [walk] [verbose]` – the knight's attack moves in the physics sim (torso / hand tracking, blade direction with `BLADE=1`); `[RIG=skeleton|robot] node tools/atk-check.mjs [kind ...]` – reach + joint limits of every move; `RIG=skeleton node tools/roll-check.mjs` – the hand's turn about the forearm (ghost vs physical); `node tools/torque-log.mjs [punch|sword|...]` – which controller torques the chest when it spins fast; `node tools/hand-analyze.mjs skeleton.glb` – splits a hand mesh into palm / finger / thumb parts
- `node tools/build-profile.mjs Untitled.glb src/profile.json` / `... skeleton.glb src/profile-skeleton.json skeleton` / `... robot.glb src/profile-robot.json robot` – re-fit colliders if a model changes; `view.html?m=skeleton.glb&fits=1&bones=1&cam=x,y,z&look=x,y,z` shows a model with its fitted colliders / bones
- Browser debug: `?film=ghost` / `?film=sim&dir=0..3` render a filmstrip of the whole get-up; `?film=sword` (kinematic) / `?film=swordsim[&sheathe=1]` (physics) the sword draw (`&ps=` progress marks, `&cam=&look=&fov=`); `?film=grip` the fist on the grip; `?film=attack&mode=punch|sword[&heavy=1][&hold=1][&skip=s][&dt=s][&n=]` (add `&rig=skeleton|robot`) the attack moves; `&kind=uppercut[&side=R]` plays one named move, `&key=Q` presses a special button; `&fist=0..1` forces the finger curl; `?film=walk` (kinematic) / `?film=walksim[&sword=1][&thr=0..1]` (physics) the gait cycle (`&rig=skeleton|robot`, `&run=1` running, `&swing=1[&kinds=slash|hook|smash]` savage swings, `&dt=0.1` seconds between frames, `&n=` frames); `?film=jump&rig=robot[&dir=up|leap|run]` the robot's jump (`&dt=`, `&n=`); `&col=1` shows the collision shapes, `&hide=1` hides the sword meshes; `window.__app.advance(seconds)` steps the sim without animation frames (the in-app browser pauses rAF while hidden).
